CampusHira Grading Policy

Student academic performance is evaluated using marks obtained in academic assessments.

Marks may be awarded for internal assessments, examinations, assignments, practical activities, and other academic components depending on the subject.

A student's percentage can be calculated as:

Percentage = (Marks Obtained / Maximum Marks) × 100

Students should regularly check their marks and report discrepancies to the appropriate faculty member or academic office.

The exact grading structure may vary depending on the course and academic regulations.
