CampusHira Academic Rules

Students are expected to regularly monitor their attendance, marks, timetable, examination information, and academic notices.

Students should maintain the minimum attendance requirement specified by the institution.

Students should follow academic schedules and attend classes regularly.

Students should review official notices for changes to examinations, classes, academic activities, and deadlines.

For questions about student-specific academic information, the CampusHira ERP database should be treated as the source of the student's personal academic records.

For official academic regulations, students should refer to the relevant institutional policy or contact the appropriate academic office.
