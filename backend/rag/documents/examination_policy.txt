CampusHira Examination Policy

Examinations are conducted according to the academic calendar of the institution.

Students must attend examinations at the scheduled date and time.

Students should carry their valid college identification when required for examinations.

Internal assessments may include tests, assignments, practical work, presentations, or other academic activities.

Students are responsible for checking examination schedules and notices published by the institution.

If a student is absent for an examination, the student should follow the examination cell's procedures regarding absence, permission, or supplementary examinations.

Students should contact the examination cell for official clarification regarding examination rules.
