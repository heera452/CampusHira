CAMPUSHIRA COLLEGE ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Minimum Attendance Requirement

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each subject.

2. Attendance Shortage

Students whose attendance falls below 75% may be required to submit a shortage explanation and follow the college's attendance shortage procedure.

3. Medical Leave

Medical leave may be considered according to the college rules and requires appropriate supporting documentation.

4. Attendance Calculation

Attendance percentage is calculated as:

Attendance Percentage = (Classes Attended / Total Classes) × 100

5. Student Responsibility

Students are responsible for regularly checking their attendance records and reporting discrepancies to the concerned faculty member.

6. Academic Risk

Low attendance can affect a student's academic progress and may result in academic intervention according to college regulations.
